What to trust

Setup time is active work. Approval wait is waiting for a provider. A range means the provider does not publish one fixed number, or the time depends on your submission. Unknown means it could not be confirmed in this check, not that it is zero.

Full guides were checked against official or close to official docs on 2026-10-31. Summary guides are not verified. No sign-in, app creation, or provider approval was completed, and no secrets are included. See README for decisions and limitations.
